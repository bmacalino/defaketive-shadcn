DeFaketive

  1. Open a product on Lazada PH.
  2. Press Check with DeFaketive at the bottom left of the page.
  3. Your report opens in DeFaketive: in its tab if you have one open, or in a new tab. You need a free DeFaketive account.

Reports show what buyers wrote about safety problems, faults and fakes. They are not proof.
